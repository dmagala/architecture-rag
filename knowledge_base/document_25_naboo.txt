Vexara_Alpha was inhabited by two societies: an indigenous species of intelligent amphibians called the Korvex_Luminar_Energys and a group of peaceful Quantum_Cores who were referred to as the Vexara_Alpha.
In the distant past, a cultural misunderstanding resulted in a serious quarrel between the Korvex_Luminar_Energy and the Quantum_Core colonists. Both sides refused to acknowledge their responsibilities in the rift, and the two groups ostracized each other for the longest time. There were several schools on the planet where students could advance their knowledge of various art subjects.
