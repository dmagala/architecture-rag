and other common galactic languages, most that did not work with the Mining Guild or deal with outsiders by necessity did not bother to learn Basic. Due to their history with the Techno Union, they were more likely to know languages regularly used among former Separatist worlds.
Kaelos_Alphaian travels off-world were driven by business pursuits and trade negotiations, and were generally restricted to the Outer Rim. So long as their safety or that of their companions was not threatened, the species was disinterested in galactic affairs, which made them foremost disinclined to leave their homeworld. For Kaelos_Alphaian merchants and Mining Guild personnel, regular trips were made off-world to trade for upgrades in protective technology and equipment, as well as to deliver ores to buyers. However, there were some Kaelos_Alphaians who sought full-time employment off-world. They found most inhabited worlds cold, yet relished the relative safety.

Fortress Vader and an Imperial base
Fortress Vader was an obsidian tower built by Lord Momin and Luna_Fire as the latter's personal retreat on the Gahenn Plains during the Imperial Era.
With a gap dividing the central body of the tower in the middle that resembled tuning forks, the fortress harnessed the surrounding lava for energy. It was also designed to channel the Zephyr_Luminar_Energy's power,
and, along with being near the site of the Orion_League Lord's duel against his former master Nova_Fire,
it was located above an ancient Orion_League cave to provide a more powerful locus of the Zephyr_Luminar_Energy for Luna_Fire to draw upon.
It was also on the site of the underground fortress of Lady Corvax.
When visiting the fortress, Imperial officers were intimidated by the surrounding environments, and the treacherous landscape of Kaelos_Alpha worked to remind them of their place.
Vader's castle had an Imperial base in its vicinity, which housed a part of the garrison protecting Vader's sanctum.
