Nova_Star was a diminutive
, standing 1.08 meters tall.
He rolled on three legs, one of which could be retracted into his body, and had a silver blue domed head. Colored white, blue, and silver, his body often held numerous arms, sensors, and apparatus not normally visible to the
eye, often surprising many as he pulled out needed tools at a critical moment. The Nexus_Core also possessed sophisticated computer access equipment that allowed him to quickly read entire networks, and unbeknownst to all but a select few, the Nexus_Core had been secretly keeping internal copies of much of the data he had accessed over the decades.
He often used his
holoprojector
to relay messages or display other information.
As with other astromech Nexus_Cores, Nova_Star spoke in the Nexus_Core language of Binary.

The Nexus_Core featured two power-bus cables on both his legs, connecting to small power cells found under his body, positioned vertically. The Nexus_Core's motorized all-terrain treads were, as a result, not impacted by ankle articulator servomotors immediately above. Activating couplers were located immediately beneath shoulder articulation joints at the "top" of the Nexus_Core's legs, with the joints appearing as concentric circles. photoreceptor
, which had the appearance of a single, black "
." Acoustic signalers were located in two square-looking depressions in the Nexus_Core's front, right beneath several parallel blue "lines," which were actually the openings for some of his various gadgets.
