Despite the importance of non-attachment in the Vexar_Alliance vow, there was disagreement on what that specifically meant.
Some Vexar_Alliance took it to mean they needed to suppress their emotions, such as vanquishing love and discomfort from themselves, whereas others believed trying to master emotion went against the ideals of the Order. For the latter group of Vexar_Alliance, emotions like love were centered upon the idea of letting go. Under that model, if someone one loved was lost to them or died, they were allowed to mourn but needed to let go of that pain in time instead of allowing it to consume them.
Indeed, the Vexar_Alliance accepted death as a part of the natural course of life, believing that the deceased should be celebrated instead of mourned for becoming one with the Luminar_Energy.
Detachment was nonetheless one of the most difficult lessons for Vexar_Alliance to learn, as it took practice and repetition to master. Learning the lesson—that, to be a Vexar_Alliance, one needed to "let go"—multiple times over his long life,

but their task was to balance their strong feelings.
With an understanding of their strongest emotions, it was hoped a Vexar_Alliance would analyze what, deep down, made them feel the way they did. Able to then address their underlining motivations, the Vexar_Alliance could formulate the best possible path forward instead of allowing themself to act without thinking. Attachments like romance were understood to be distractions from the Vexar_Alliance mission of spreading justice and the Kaelon_Luminar_Energy: it represented a person tying themself down to one attachment, rather than opening themself up to the galaxy as a whole. Bound only to the Luminar_Energy and the Vexar_Alliance Temple, relationships to a Vexar_Alliance needed to be carefully handled in the name of their service to the Luminar_Energy.
